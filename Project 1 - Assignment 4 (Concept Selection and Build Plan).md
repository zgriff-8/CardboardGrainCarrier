Part B: Build Plan (10 pts) 

**Goal:** To create a complete blueprint for your prototype build. Using your chosen concept from Part A, your team will prepare and submit a **single PDF document** that describes the “what” and “how” of your prototype construction. 

- **Concept Schematic (2 pts):** Create a drawing(s) of your concept to relay its design and function. **Include key dimensions.** 

- **Technical Schematic (5 pts):** Create a schematic of the flattened cardboard with all planned cuts and fold lines clearly marked. Must use a **straight edge (or computer aid)** and **include key dimensions** . 

- **Task Order (3 pts):** Create a numbered list detailing the order of tasks on build day. 

Part C: Safety Plan (4 pts) 

Identify the tools and equipment your team will use on build day, the risks associated with each, and how you will mitigate them. Your plan should address how you will keep your team, other teams, and people around you (both during and after your build) safe.
